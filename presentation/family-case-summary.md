# Family / Case Summary

## Purpose
This is the optional narrative lead document behind the thin README. It reconstructs the present family/case model without treating either parent's litigation position as established fact.

## Family reality presently supported
Rayan and Aleena have meaningful relationships with both parents and have spent years in a substantially shared arrangement. The current record does not presently establish that shared possession itself is failing. It does establish recurring adult conflict around custody, possession, authority, activities, and the children's relationships with their parents.

Sana sought greater or primary custody before the April 15, 2021 order and expressed opposition to equal possession before many current alleged conditions existed. Many present allegation categories also predate the order. That makes chronology central: identify what actually changed after 4/15/21 rather than treating repetition of an old dispute as a new condition.

Rayan's 2025–26 behavioral deterioration is a genuinely newer, objectively documented condition. School records document attendance/discipline problems, fights, suspensions, disruption, and missing work. The evidence reviewed so far does not establish that Eric, Eric's household, or shared possession caused that deterioration, nor that reducing Eric's possession would remedy it.

The record also contains substantial evidence that the children experience adult parental conflict. Individual events vary in proof quality: some are neutral-professional observations, some child-authored writings or messages, some parent reports, and some disputed allegations. Those categories must remain separate.

## Eric's stated objective
Eric does not seek to remove Sana from the children's lives. His stated objective is a peaceful childhood, meaningful and flexible access to both parents, and a structure that prevents adult conflict from controlling ordinary childhood relationships and decisions. If the current structure can accomplish that, preserve it; if additional authority is required, use it to protect both parent-child relationships.

## Sana's position — incomplete pending discovery
Historical communications show a longstanding desire for greater/primary custody and repeated concerns about Eric's parenting, judgment, safety, school/medical involvement, decision-making, co-parenting, substance/gambling themes, and equal possession. The repository should not assume those historical statements fully describe Sana's current case. Written discovery should require her to identify the exact post-4/15/21 changes, evidence, claimed child impact, causal theory, requested relief, and remedy nexus.

## Current working conclusion
The evidence presently points toward a parenting schedule that is more functional than the litigation narrative suggests, with conflict surrounding the schedule increasingly entering the children's world. That conclusion is provisional.

The model should change if reliable evidence establishes a new post-order condition caused by Eric that materially harms the children; neutral evidence ties Eric/shared possession to Rayan's deterioration; the schedule itself is shown to harm the children; Sana's requested modification is shown likely to improve their circumstances; or another explanation better fits the evidence.

**Winning an inaccurate model is not the objective. Finding the accurate model is.**
