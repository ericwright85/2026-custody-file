# Legal decision rules — attorney input requested

This page is intentionally short. The factual record is being indexed separately. This page is for **Texas-law decision rules** that should govern how the evidence is evaluated.

## Questions for counsel

Please add or correct the legal standards you want the evidence model to apply, especially:

1. **Material and substantial change**
   - What is the controlling standard in this court/case posture?
   - What facts are legally capable of qualifying?
   - How should facts that existed before the 4/15/2021 order be treated?

2. **Best interest / modification nexus**
   - Once a change is shown, what must connect that change to the requested modification?
   - How important is evidence that the proposed remedy would actually address the claimed problem?

3. **Possession modification**
   - What standards or presumptions matter when moving away from an established approximately equal-possession arrangement?
   - What weight should be given to a schedule that is functioning despite high parental conflict?

4. **Conservatorship / exclusive rights**
   - What must be shown to justify giving one parent exclusive educational, medical, psychological, or residence-designation rights?
   - What evidence tends to matter most: unilateral decisions, provider conflict, child outcomes, inability to agree, or something else?

5. **Child preference / adolescent behavior**
   - How should a 12/13-year-old child's expressed preferences be used?
   - What is the distinction between the child's preference, the child's behavioral deterioration, and proof that a particular parent or schedule caused it?

6. **Alienation / disparagement / involving children in litigation**
   - What legal framework should be used for these facts?
   - Which behaviors matter without requiring a clinical "parental alienation" diagnosis?

7. **Enforcement / decree violations**
   - What elements must be proved for each enforcement claim?
   - What exact decree language, notice, intent, and proof should be indexed for contempt/enforcement purposes?

8. **Evidence / admissibility priorities**
   - Which categories need authentication or hearsay exceptions before we rely on them heavily: child texts, journal entries, therapist emails, recordings, school records, TalkingParents, screenshots, provider statements?

## How this will be used

Once counsel supplies the governing rules, each major allegation can be evaluated through the same pipeline:

`claimed change → date / pre-existing? → objective proof → child impact → causation → requested remedy → legal sufficiency`

The goal is not to replace legal judgment. It is to make the factual record searchable and to surface the evidence that matters under the legal rules counsel identifies.
