# Proposed Interrogatories

### Interrogatory 1 — Requested relief
Identify each modification Sana seeks concerning conservatorship, possession, access, parental rights or duties, decision-making authority, geographic restriction, child support, or other child-related relief, and state the factual basis for each requested modification.

### Interrogatory 2 — Material changes after April 15, 2021
Identify each material and substantial change in circumstances occurring after April 15, 2021 that Sana contends supports modification of the existing order. For each, state when it began, what changed, and the facts upon which Sana relies in contending that the condition is new, material, and substantial.

### Interrogatory 3 — Evidence and persons with knowledge
For each alleged material change identified above, identify the persons with knowledge and the documents, communications, recordings, photographs, videos, records, or other evidence Sana contends support it.

### Interrogatory 4 — Child harm attributed to Eric or shared possession
Identify each behavioral, emotional, educational, medical, safety, developmental, or other adverse condition affecting Rayan or Aleena that Sana contends was caused or materially contributed to by Eric, Eric's household, or the existing possession arrangement. For each, state the claimed cause and factual basis for that causal connection.

### Interrogatory 5 — Remedy nexus
For each adverse condition Sana contends supports modification, identify the specific requested modification Sana contends would alleviate or remedy the condition and state the factual basis for believing that modification would improve the child's circumstances or best interest.

### Interrogatory 6 — Professionals and neutral third parties
Identify each counselor, therapist, physician, psychologist, evaluator, teacher, administrator, coach, activity leader, law-enforcement officer, CPS employee, or other professional or neutral third party whom Sana contends possesses information supporting an alleged material change, alleged child harm, criticism of Eric's parenting or the existing possession arrangement, or Sana's requested modification, and describe the information or opinion Sana believes the person possesses.

### Interrogatory 7 — Pre-order existence of current concerns
For each concern regarding Eric's parenting, conduct, household, decision-making, safety, supervision, substance use, school involvement, medical involvement, communication, co-parenting, or possession arrangement upon which Sana presently relies, state whether Sana held or communicated the same or a substantially similar concern before April 15, 2021 and, if so, approximately when it first arose or was communicated.

### Interrogatory 8 — Claimed harm from the possession schedule itself
Identify each fact upon which Sana relies in contending that the existing possession arrangement itself is harmful, unworkable, or contrary to either child's best interest. Distinguish, if possible, harm attributed to the structure of the schedule from harm attributed to conflict or disagreement between the parents.

### Interrogatory 9 — Rayan's 2025–26 deterioration
State the factual basis for any contention that Eric, Eric's household, transitions between households, or the existing possession arrangement caused or materially contributed to Rayan's 2025–26 attendance, discipline, behavioral, emotional, or school difficulties. Identify any claimed clustering by household, possession period, exchange, or transition and the evidence supporting that contention.

### Interrogatory 10 — Child statements relied upon
Identify each statement by Rayan or Aleena upon which Sana relies in seeking modification, including the approximate date, circumstances, persons present, and whether the statement followed discussion or questioning concerning custody, possession, residence, parental preference, or either parent.

### Interrogatory 11 — Communications with children about litigation
Identify communications Sana has had with Rayan or Aleena concerning this litigation, custody or modification, possession schedules, court, attorneys, mediation, evidence or recordings, or what either child wants a court to decide. Identify any occasion on which Sana asked or encouraged either child to create, record, photograph, write, or communicate material concerning either parent, custody, possession, residence, parental preference, or the litigation.

### Interrogatory 12 — Alleged alienation, disparagement, or interference by Eric
Identify each specific instance after April 15, 2021 in which Sana contends Eric disparaged Sana to either child, interfered with Sana's relationship with either child, encouraged either child to reject Sana, improperly involved a child in adult conflict, or otherwise attempted to alienate either child from Sana. For each, state the approximate date, conduct alleged, source of Sana's knowledge, and resulting child impact claimed.

### Interrogatory 13 — Safety / supervision / substance allegations
Identify each specific instance after April 15, 2021 in which Sana contends either child was unsafe, inadequately supervised, medically neglected, exposed to impairment or inappropriate substance use, or otherwise endangered while in Eric's care. For each, state the approximate date, conduct alleged, source of Sana's knowledge, and resulting harm claimed.

### Interrogatory 14 — Joint-right / school / medical / activity allegations
Identify each specific post-April 15, 2021 decision or action by Eric that Sana contends violated her parental rights or materially harmed a child concerning education, medical or mental-health care, childcare, transportation, extracurricular activities, or another significant child-related decision. Identify the right allegedly violated and resulting child impact claimed.

### Interrogatory 15 — Post-mediation communications with Rayan
Identify communications or actions by Sana after learning that recordings made or provided by Rayan had been used or discussed in mediation concerning Sana's trust in Rayan, his key or access to Sana's residence, whether he should return to or come to Sana's residence, Sana's attendance at his football activities, or his relationship with Sana.

**Strategy flag:** counsel should decide whether Interrogatory 15 and other impeachment-sensitive subjects should instead be preserved for deposition.

# Proposed Requests for Production

### RFP 1 — Evidence supporting requested modification
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends support any requested modification identified in her pleadings or interrogatory responses.

### RFP 2 — Evidence supporting alleged material changes
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends support each alleged material and substantial change occurring after April 15, 2021.

### RFP 3 — Child harm / causation evidence
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends support any allegation that Eric, Eric's household, or the existing possession arrangement caused or materially contributed to an adverse condition affecting either child.

### RFP 4 — Remedy-nexus evidence
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends support the proposition that a requested change in Eric's possession, rights, authority, or decision-making would alleviate an identified problem or improve either child's circumstances.

### RFP 5 — Professional communications
Produce nonprivileged communications with counselors, therapists, physicians, psychologists, evaluators, teachers, administrators, coaches, or other professionals concerning Eric's parenting, either child's relationship with Eric, the existing possession arrangement, alleged child harm, custody, or modification, subject to counsel's appropriate privacy and privilege limitations.

### RFP 6 — Child / litigation communications
Produce nonprivileged materials reflecting communications between Sana and either child concerning litigation, custody, modification, possession, court, attorneys, mediation, evidence or recordings, residence, parental preference, or what a court should decide.

### RFP 7 — Child-created materials
Produce videos, audio recordings, writings, photographs, messages, statements, or other materials created by either child at Sana's request or encouragement concerning either parent, custody, possession, residence, parental preference, or litigation.

### RFP 8 — Alleged alienation / disparagement evidence
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends support allegations that Eric disparaged Sana, interfered with her relationship with either child, encouraged rejection, improperly involved a child in adult conflict, or attempted to alienate either child.

### RFP 9 — Safety / supervision / substance evidence
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends support any post-April 15, 2021 allegation involving unsafe supervision, neglect, impairment, substance exposure, or other endangerment while either child was in Eric's care.

### RFP 10 — School / attendance / discipline evidence
Produce school, attendance, discipline, academic, teacher, administrator, counselor, or related materials upon which Sana relies to attribute either child's difficulties to Eric, Eric's household, transitions, or the existing possession arrangement.

### RFP 11 — Joint-right / unilateral-decision evidence
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends demonstrate that Eric improperly exercised or violated parental decision rights regarding education, medical or mental-health care, childcare, transportation, extracurricular activities, or other significant child-related decisions.

### RFP 12 — Schedule-harm evidence
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana contends demonstrate that the structure of the existing possession schedule itself, as distinguished from parental conflict surrounding the schedule, has harmed either child or is unworkable.

### RFP 13 — Current custody-outcome basis
Produce nonprivileged materials upon which Sana relies for her present contention that Eric's possession should be reduced, equal/shared possession should end, or Sana should receive greater possession or decision-making authority.

### RFP 14 — Post-mediation Rayan materials
Produce nonprivileged materials concerning the post-mediation communications or actions involving Rayan described in Interrogatory 15, if counsel elects to discover this subject in writing rather than preserve it for deposition.

### RFP 15 — Materials intended to support Sana's modification case
Produce nonprivileged materials Sana presently expects to rely upon to support the requested modification, to the extent discoverable and not previously produced.
