# Sana Deposition Preparation — working outline for counsel

> **Purpose:** organize the factual questions counsel may want answered under oath. This is not a script and does not replace counsel's judgment about Texas procedure, objections, admissibility, privilege, or sequencing.

The central objective is to leave the deposition with a clear record of **what Sana says changed, when it changed, what facts support each claim, what effect she says it had on the children, and why she believes her requested modification addresses it.**

---

## 1. Start with the requested outcome

Questions to clarify:

- What exact custody / possession / conservatorship changes is Sana requesting?
- Which requested changes concern possession time?
- Which concern decision-making rights?
- Which concern residence designation, medical, educational, psychological, or extracurricular authority?
- What child-centered problem does each requested change solve?
- When did she first decide each change was necessary?

## 2. Define the alleged material change

For every condition she relies on:

- What exactly changed after **4/15/2021**?
- On what date or approximate period did it change?
- Was any version of that concern present before 4/15/2021?
- Did she communicate the same concern before the order?
- What makes the post-order condition qualitatively different?
- What objective evidence supports that difference?

The goal is to distinguish:

`pre-existing concern / continuing concern / genuinely new condition / worsening condition / newly discovered evidence`

[→ Claim-by-claim material-change matrix](../analysis/claim-by-claim-material-change-matrix.md)

---

## 3. Establish the basis for each allegation

For each allegation, counsel may want to separate:

- what Sana personally observed;
- what a child told her;
- what a teacher/provider told her;
- what she inferred;
- what appears in a document;
- what another person told her;
- what she cannot independently corroborate.

For each item:

1. What happened?
2. When?
3. Who was present?
4. Who has firsthand knowledge?
5. Is there a document, message, photo, recording, provider note, school record, or witness?
6. What part is disputed?

This can later be compared directly with the [clickable evidence registry](../index/evidence-links.md).

---

## 4. The custody objective chronology

Clarify Sana's historical custody position, including the documented statements that she deserved primary / more custody or that Eric did not deserve 50/50.

Potential factual questions:

- When did she first believe equal possession was inappropriate?
- Did that belief predate 4/15/2021?
- What facts supported it at that time?
- Which of those facts are the same facts relied upon now?
- What changed between those earlier demands and the current modification request?
- What did she mean by statements that she `deserved` more time or that Eric did not `deserve` 50/50?

[→ “Deserve” chronology](../index/deserve-language.md)

**Purpose:** chronology and meaning, not psychological diagnosis.

---

## 5. Does the current schedule actually fail?

Clarify the claimed functional problems with the approximately equal schedule:

- Which specific child outcomes does she attribute to the schedule?
- What objective evidence links those outcomes to transitions or equal possession?
- Were the children's grades, attendance, activities, medical care, or relationships functioning during earlier periods of the same schedule?
- What parts of the schedule work?
- What parts do not?
- Has she agreed to swaps or deviations when useful?
- What does she believe would improve if Eric had less time?

The important distinction is:

`parental conflict around a schedule ≠ proof that the schedule itself harms the children`

---

## 6. Rayan's 2025–26 behavior deterioration

This should probably be treated as a real factual condition, not denied.

Clarify:

- What does Sana believe caused the deterioration?
- What evidence supports that causal belief?
- Does any teacher, counselor, therapist, evaluator, or physician attribute it to Eric or equal possession?
- Do incidents cluster around either household, transitions, school peers, discipline events, or other factors?
- What interventions were attempted?
- Which interventions helped?
- How would reducing Eric's possession address the behavior?
- Does Sana contend the problem is academic, behavioral, emotional, disciplinary, or some combination?

[→ School review](../reviews/2025-2026-rayan-school-records.md)  
[→ Causation analysis](../analysis/rayan-behavior-causation-hypotheses.md)

## 7. Heidi Zimmerman / counseling history

Factual areas to clarify:

- Why was Heidi selected?
- What did Sana understand Heidi's role to be?
- Did Sana request a custody-related recommendation or letter?
- What response did Heidi give?
- Why did Sana lose confidence in Heidi?
- Who decided treatment would stop / change?
- What did Sana understand Rayan was reporting to Heidi about parental conflict?
- What concerns, if any, did Heidi communicate about Eric?
- What concerns, if any, did Heidi communicate about Sana?

[→ Heidi review](../reviews/heidi-zimmerman-evidence.md)  
[→ Direct proof links](../index/evidence-links.md#heidi-zimmerman--neutral-counselor-cluster)

The neutral framing matters: Heidi also criticized Eric. The issue is what the counselor actually documented, not whose `side` she was on.

---

## 8. Child involvement in adult conflict

Clarify specific alleged or documented incidents rather than asking Sana to accept labels such as `alienation`.

Potential areas:

- adult accusations communicated in front of / to the children;
- children being asked to carry messages;
- discussion of custody, litigation, child support, or the other parent's alleged conduct;
- asking children to state or record custody preferences;
- statements suggesting a child need not return to the other home;
- restrictions on child-parent communication during conflict;
- statements about one parent being bad / dishonest / unsafe.

For each incident: exact words, date, context, purpose, child reaction, and corroboration.

---

## 9. Rayan's reports about discipline / rejection / fear

Where counsel believes the evidence is usable, clarify Sana's account of the underlying events rather than assuming the child's account is independently verified.

Current items include reports concerning:

- being told he is `bad` / love being withdrawn when he is bad;
- fear of Mom during conflict;
- being told to get out / not return;
- repeated slapping allegation;
- device/contact restrictions;
- feeling that Mom is `making me feel bad every second`.

[→ Rayan message review](../reviews/rayan-text-message-evidence.md)

Questions should distinguish:

`what happened / what discipline was imposed / why / exact wording / whether contact with Eric was restricted / whether Sana disputes the child's account`

---

## 10. Historical `bad seed` / child-labeling evidence

Clarify whether Sana recalls calling Rayan a `bad seed`, the context, and whether she disputes the child-authored journal account.

Also clarify comparative labeling or treatment of Rayan and Aleena if relevant and supported.

[→ Young Rayan journals](../reviews/young-rayan-journal-entries.md)

Important: original journal-writing dates remain uncertain and should not be overstated.

---

## 11. Gymnastics / taekwondo / extracurricular participation

Clarify:

- attendance expectations;
- who transported the children;
- why practices/classes were missed;
- whether Eric offered transportation during Sana's possession;
- why those offers were accepted/rejected;
- what Sana meant when she linked continued extracurricular participation to having more time with the children;
- whether activities were ever intentionally withheld because of custody conflict;
- whether the children wanted to continue.

[→ Extracurricular review](../reviews/extracurricular-gymnastics-taekwondo-pattern.md)

This module is strongest as **conduct + chronology**, not motive speculation.

---

## 12. Specific safety / substance / responsibility allegations

For each claimed safety, marijuana, gambling, supervision, driving/e-bike, medical, educational, or responsibility concern:

- exact event;
- date;
- firsthand witness;
- documentary corroboration;
- whether it occurred before 4/15/2021 too;
- actual child harm, if any;
- whether any professional recommended reducing Eric's time because of it;
- whether Sana allowed ordinary or additional possession after the alleged event.

[→ Allegation matrix](../index/allegations.md)

## 13. Positive / inconsistent parenting assessments

Where relevant to a claim that Eric has historically been unsafe, uninvolved, or incapable, clarify Sana's own prior assessments:

- 2013: belief Eric would be a `great daddy` and recognition of his love/care toward Rayan;
- 2017: statement that he was great with Aleena and cared for her;
- 2018: statement about how he cared for Sana and the kids every day and his loving nature.

[→ Historical relationship email review](../reviews/historical-relationship-email-evidence.md)  
[→ Gmail source links](../index/evidence-links.md#historical-relationship--conflict-state-evidence)

The question is not whether views can change. It is **when, why, and based on what new facts** they changed.

## 14. Historical conflict-state admissions / Whiteaker context

Counsel can decide whether this is relevant enough to explore.

Documented historical points include Sana's 2014 statements that she:

- `said something to hurt` Eric;
- acted `out of spite`;
- distinguished fight language from what she believed about him at his core;
- acknowledged: `for that reason I used Rayan.`

The record also corroborates marriage counseling with Dr. David Whiteaker. Eric later memorialized in 2022 that marriage counseling had addressed a `fear of abandonment` and behavior that could `promote abandonment`.

Potential questions should be factual:

- Does Sana recall the 2014 email and what she meant?
- Does she recall counseling with Whiteaker?
- Does she recall discussions concerning abandonment / rejection / pushing people away?
- Does she recall discussing statements made when angry versus later-regulated views?

Do **not** ask her to accept a diagnosis or turn the deposition into amateur psychology.

[→ Historical evidence review](../reviews/historical-relationship-email-evidence.md)

## 15. Eric supporting the children's relationship with Sana

Clarify whether Sana disputes documented examples of Eric:

- encouraging children to love/respect Mom;
- offering Mom extra contact / lunches / flexibility;
- supporting shared activities and family contact;
- reassuring a child who believed Mom was rejecting him that she did not really mean abandonment;
- offering transportation to preserve children's activities during her possession.

This matters if the case includes allegations that Eric seeks to estrange the children from Sana.

---

## 16. Remedy questions — the end of every major module

After establishing an allegation, ask the practical question:

> **What exact modification does Sana say should result from this fact, and why?**

Examples:

- Why does a school fight support less time with Eric?
- Why does a communication dispute support changing possession?
- Why does an extracurricular disagreement support changing conservatorship?
- If a problem existed before 4/15/2021, what new feature now requires a different order?
- If the children need peace and flexibility, how does the requested order create those conditions?

This is not merely a credibility inquiry. It forces the factual theory and requested remedy to meet.

# Suggested counsel workflow during deposition prep

For each module, prepare a one-page sheet:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Proposition to clarify | What do we need to know? |
| Sana's known prior statement | TalkingParents / pleading / discovery response |
| Evidence | Source ID + direct link |
| Questions | Open factual questions first, narrower follow-ups second |
| Unknown | What answer would materially change our case view? |
| Legal significance | Bruman to supply |

A productive deposition is one that **reduces uncertainty**. If Sana has strong evidence we do not know about, we want it identified. If she does not, the transcript should make the limits of the claim clear.
