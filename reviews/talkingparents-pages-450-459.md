# TalkingParents review — pages 450–459

Source: `talking parents record(3).pdf`, pp. 450–459.

## Strongest points
- 12/7/2021 campout/schedule dispute: Sana withdrew the previously agreed weekend swap unless Eric accepted her newly proposed schedule. Eric ultimately agreed to her proposed schedule so Rayan could attend the campout. This supports the theme that Eric would concede disputed parent-time accounting to preserve a child activity, but his tone toward Sana was poor.
- 12/4/2021 school holiday event: Eric did not know about the 9am–noon family event and Sana raised it after it was over. Eric says proactive notice would have avoided the issue. Useful only as a communication example, not a major event.
- 7/2/2021 dentist: Eric affirmatively informed Sana of both children’s dental appointments and asked whether she planned to attend or wanted him to reschedule.
- 6/24/2021 custody dispute: this block records the competing interpretations of the summer Thursday transfer. Sana says Eric kept the children on her time the prior week; Eric says they were available at his home from 8am and she chose not to pick them up. On 6/24 Eric says Sana did not have the children at her home at 8am, he could not find them at KRK, and she told him she would not transfer them until 4pm. The texts establish a real possession dispute, but not which legal interpretation was correct without the decree/other records.
- 6/16/2021: before the next Thursday transfer, Eric offered multiple practical alternatives — earlier/later pickups, 6pm/7am, or suspending the Wed/Thu summer transfer entirely — while stating the default interpretation he believed applied. Good evidence for “propose flexibility before enforcement.”

## Adverse / caution
- Medical/behavioral consultation (11/29–12/6/2021): Sana repeatedly asks why Eric scheduled a pediatric behavioral consultation and says she was not informed/included. Eric gives only terse replies and later says his individual consultation was sufficient. This is a meaningful adverse co-parenting fact because his responses do not substantively address her request for the reason for the appointment.
- 6/24/2021: Eric used highly escalatory language (“collusion hiding my children,” “bullshit,” attacks on Sana as a mother/person). Preserve as tone/adverse evidence even if his custody-position interpretation was correct.
- 12/7/2021: Eric’s “Poor Rayan... emotional fit” framing is unnecessary and weakens the otherwise useful fact that he accepted Sana’s proposal so the campout could proceed.
