# TalkingParents review — pages 440–449

Source: `talking parents record(3).pdf`, pages 440–449.

## Strongest favorable / useful items

- **Health-insurance disclosure:** On 12/28/2021 Eric furnished employer, medical/dental carrier information, benefit documents, and membership cards after Sana requested the information. The record shows Sana downloaded the materials. This is useful as clean compliance / information-sharing evidence.
- **Campout swap cooperation:** The Nov–Dec 2021 scouting thread begins with Eric proposing a swap so Rayan could attend both winter campouts, explicitly saying the children seeing the parents cooperate would be better. After back-and-forth, the parties reached agreement on the December and January swaps. This is useful evidence that Eric proposed flexibility beyond the base schedule to support Rayan's activities.
- **Child-centered stated rationale:** Eric repeatedly frames the swap around Rayan wanting to attend the campout and preserving his extracurricular participation, including asking whether there was anything he could do to make it happen.

Eric's quoted email response is dismissive rather than substantively addressing whether he had joint-decision authority. Treat as an unresolved but potentially meaningful joint-conservatorship issue.

Sana presents a last-minute revised proposal; Eric characterizes it as threatening Rayan's campout unless he accepts an unequal schedule. The texts support that the swap became conditioned on agreement over revised possession terms, but the record does not by itself establish bad faith or a violation by Sana.
- **Marijuana allegation:** Sana accuses Eric of marijuana exposure/addiction in the 12/17–12/25/2021 thread; Eric denies it. This block does not corroborate the allegation, so preserve as allegation only.
- **Family-member comment allegation:** Sana says Eric told Rayan something was wrong with his maternal grandfather's brain. Eric simply denies it; no corroboration in this block.
