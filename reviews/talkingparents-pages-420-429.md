# TalkingParents review — pages 420–429

Source: `talking parents record(3).pdf`, pages 420–429.

## Main findings

### Rayan KRK transportation dispute — Jan–Mar 2022
- Eric documented that Rayan wanted to move from Campus Kids to Kids R Kids (KRK) and set up KRK bus transportation, initially paying both programs while testing the change.
- Sana objected that the change was made without her agreement and repeatedly framed transportation as a joint educational decision under the decree.
- The disagreement escalated to involvement by Lakewood Elementary's principal, with Sana saying the principal would choose car rider if the parents could not agree.
- This is an adverse/mixed item for Eric because the transportation change was implemented before Sana agreed, even though Eric consistently framed it as responding to Rayan's stated preference.

### Child voice / preference
- Eric repeatedly stated that Rayan wanted KRK and characterized the decision as giving Rayan a voice.
- Useful for the theme that Eric emphasizes child preference, but the surrounding language is often accusatory and weakens the presentation.

### Illness communication — Jan. 28, 2022
- Eric had already noted that Rayan was staying home after vomiting and that he would monitor him and seek pediatric care if needed.
- Sana later complained she was not adequately informed, called several times, and said she would have liked to attend the pediatric appointment.
- Eric eventually reported that Rayan was okay, had probiotics and anti-vomiting medication, and should return to school 24 hours after his last vomiting episode.
- Mixed: there is evidence Eric communicated the illness and later details, but there is also a fair co-parenting criticism that Sana did not receive appointment notice before it occurred.

### Tone / litigation risk
- Eric repeatedly used psychological labels and sweeping motive claims against Sana throughout the KRK dispute. Preserve as adverse tone evidence; do not treat those labels as established diagnoses.
