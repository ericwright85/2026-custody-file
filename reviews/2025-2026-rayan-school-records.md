## Material-change significance

This is one of the strongest genuinely new post-4/15/2021 evidence clusters located so far. The school records establish that Rayan developed substantial adolescent behavior/discipline issues by 2025–2026.

However, the records currently establish **existence of the new problem**, not **causation**. They do not show that:
- the 50/50 schedule caused the behavior;
- Eric caused the behavior;
- the conduct occurs predominantly after Eric's possession;
- reducing Eric's possession would address the problem.

The evidence therefore strengthens the argument that there is a new child-development / school-behavior condition requiring attention, while leaving the remedy and causal attribution unresolved.

## Defense/context analysis

The defensible position should not minimize the seriousness of the school behavior. A better framing is:
1. acknowledge the objectively documented adolescent behavior problem;
2. separate that problem from unsupported assumptions about household causation;
3. show both parents' involvement in school meetings/interventions;
4. compare discipline dates to actual possession history before accepting any parent-specific narrative;
5. distinguish behavioral deterioration from academic collapse, because the reviewed grade report shows passing grades and promotion.
