# TalkingParents systematic review — pp.330–339

Source: `talking parents record(3).pdf`, pp.330–339.

## Strongest findings

### 1. 3/16/2023 — reported alienation statement
Eric contemporaneously records that Rayan told him Sana said: **“I could tell you so many things about your father that would make you hate him.”** Eric responds by telling Sana that Rayan will never hate her because she is his mother and that she will always be his mother.

Significance: potentially strong alienation/undermining evidence, but it remains Eric’s report of Rayan’s statement unless independently corroborated.

Locator: p.338.

### 2. 3/21–3/22/2023 — activity inclusion vs harassment framing
Eric asks whether Sana is taking the children to Scout/Lakewood events and later offers to add her to Pack 533 text groups and asks whether she will take Aleena to an event. Sana responds that Eric is harassing her and threatens additional legal action, also saying she has asked him not to put his hands on her.

Significance: useful for activity-inclusion theme, but the physical-contact allegation is a serious adverse item and should not be minimized.

Locator: pp.333–334.

### 3. Feb–Mar 2023 illness/truancy thread — mixed evidence
Eric documented Rayan’s fever, doctor visit, negative tests, medication and follow-up temperatures. Sana then kept him home on later days, and the parties disputed responsibility for school absence notes. Sana reported that the assistant principal said Rayan was at about 10 missed instructional days and that further absences could affect grade progression.

Significance: both parents show medical/school involvement. Eric’s tone becomes unnecessarily accusatory and dismissive of Sana’s illness claims. This is mixed, not one-sided evidence.

Locators: pp.334–338.

### 4. 3/27/2023 appointment-location dispute
Eric asks where Sana is taking Rayan for an appointment; Sana says the appointment had already been disclosed in another thread. Eric says the location was not disclosed and characterizes the refusal as parental alienation.

Significance: supports recurring communication dispute, but not strong alienation proof by itself.

Locators: pp.331–332.

## Significant adverse facts / tone

- Eric repeatedly uses psychological labels and character judgments: “narcissism,” “mental health,” “do you have a soul,” “incapable,” “only one genuinely engaged as a parent.” These are poor litigation optics and should be preserved, not hidden.
- 3/22/2023 Sana says Eric has been harassing her and that she has asked him not to put his hands on her. TP alone does not establish what physical contact occurred, but this allegation is significant and should be cross-checked for witnesses/records.
- In the truancy thread Eric repeatedly suggests Sana fabricated illness despite the earlier documented illness. That overreach could hurt credibility.

## Positive / useful counterpoints

- Eric explicitly offered Sana inclusion in Facebook Messenger controls and Scout text groups.
- Eric’s 3/16 response to the reported “make you hate him” comment reinforces that Rayan should continue loving his mother.
- The medical thread shows detailed contemporaneous symptom reporting and doctor follow-up by Eric.

## Theme classification

- Alienation / undermining dad-child bond
- Eric supports mom-child relationship
- Child activities vs possession/control
- Conflict largely Sana-generated — mixed here; tone from both sides weakens a pure one-sided thesis
- Same facts, different framing
- School/medical responsibility disputes

- Any records/witnesses concerning the 3/22/2023 allegation that Eric put his hands on Sana.
