## Strongest points
- 4/26/2021 and 5/6/2021 — immediately after the 4/15/2021 cutoff — Sana is already disputing Eric’s Thursday-morning handling of Aleena and alleging violations of the decree. This is highly relevant continuity evidence: the same transfer/daycare/co-parenting conflict appears essentially immediately after the cutoff rather than emerging later as a new condition.
- Eric repeatedly says counsel had confirmed his interpretation, though the TalkingParents record alone does not prove that legal conclusion. This should be matched to attorney correspondence/decree language later.
- 2020 school-transportation thread is useful baseline: both parents were already disputing consistency, bus vs car, routines, and what counted as child-centered decision-making well before the cutoff.
- Eric explicitly says he wants Rayan to have a positive image of his mother and wants both parents to keep him out of adult conflict. This is potentially useful support-mom-child-relationship evidence, though embedded in otherwise combative exchanges.

## Adverse / caution
- Eric’s 4/26/2021 language is particularly poor: “worthless conflict,” “bitch about stupid things,” “primary parent functionally,” and repeated claims that Sana was emotionally dysfunctional. This is strong adverse evidence on tone and cooperative co-parenting.
- In the 2020 transportation thread, Eric repeatedly insists that his accusatory language is merely “truth” rather than disparagement. That framing may be vulnerable in court because the messages themselves are plainly personal and combative.
- Sana alleges Eric called her an “evil bitch”/“bad person” in front of the children. That allegation is not corroborated in this block, so preserve as allegation only.

Very high. The 4/26/2021 dispute occurs only 11 days after the cutoff and mirrors later allegations about schedule compliance, daycare timing, communication, and co-parenting. It is strong evidence that these categories of conflict were already present at or immediately after the material-change baseline.
