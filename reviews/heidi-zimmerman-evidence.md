## Key chronology

### 2021-08-02 to 2021-08-10 — Sana asks Heidi for a primary-parent recommendation
Sana emailed Heidi after Eric filed an enforcement motion and asked Heidi to provide a professional recommendation about which parent was better fit to be the children's primary parent. Sana told Heidi that a prior evaluator had recommended Sana be primary and asserted that the 50/50 schedule was not working and was too much on the children.

Heidi declined. She wrote that she did not have enough direct insight into either parent's home life to formulate that type of letter, that time with both parents was vital to Rayan's well-being, and that she needed to remain neutral in Rayan's best interests.

Source: Google Drive, `heidi responding to sana asking for letter.pdf` (8/10/2021 email chain).

### 2021-09-04 to 2021-09-10 — Rayan asks to see Heidi; Heidi reports being caught in the middle
Eric arranged a session after Rayan asked to see Heidi. Heidi later emailed both parents that Rayan came in with his own concerns and, while tearful, said he was miserable about how things were between his parents and wanted to be able to be happy with his dad, mom, and Kaleb. Heidi reported that Rayan felt caught up in how unhappy his mother was with his father, did not like hearing negative things about his father from her, loved both parents, and did not want either parent speaking badly about the other.

Heidi recommended more regular access and said Rayan asked to see her at least monthly. She expressly invited Sana to bring him in October.

Source: Google Drive, `heidi to me -1.pdf`; `Heidi and Me - emergency session and Heidi on Sana.pdf`.

### 2021-09-09 — Sana ends counseling with Heidi
In response to Heidi's report, Sana wrote that she did not feel confident continuing counseling with Heidi and that Rayan would be introduced to another counselor. Sana alleged that Eric filled the children's heads with misinformation and said the same type of last-minute counseling need did not occur with her.

Heidi later told Eric that she believed Sana had decided to move counseling elsewhere after Heidi would not provide the requested custody recommendation. Heidi wrote that Sana had made a decision to cut Heidi out to serve her own agenda and that the decision should have been made jointly.

Source: Google Drive, same September 2021 email chains.

## Evidentiary significance

### High-value third-party corroboration
This is materially stronger than Eric merely reporting what Rayan said. Heidi was the child's treating counselor and independently reported Rayan's words and emotional presentation to both parents contemporaneously. Her email supports a child-in-the-middle / disparagement theme while also preserving the child's love for both parents.

### Potential interference with treatment / provider continuity
The documents support a fact pattern in which:
- Rayan affirmatively asked to see Heidi;
- Heidi believed continued access was beneficial and offered monthly sessions;
- Sana had previously asked Heidi for a letter favoring a primary-custody arrangement;
- Heidi declined on neutrality grounds; and
- Sana then moved to discontinue Heidi shortly after Heidi reported Rayan's concerns.

This chronology does not by itself prove an improper motive, but Heidi herself expressly stated her belief that the provider change was related to her refusal to support Sana's requested custodial outcome.

### Material-change relevance
The incident occurred after the 4/15/2021 anchor but very early in the post-decree period. It may be important in two different ways:
1. as a genuinely significant post-cutoff child-mental-health event; and
2. as evidence that the child's distress centered on parental conflict / disparagement rather than on the 50/50 schedule itself.

The second point is especially important because Heidi's reported words from Rayan were not that he wanted less time with either parent; he wanted to be happy with both parents without being caught in their conflict.

## Important limits / adverse qualifiers

- Heidi expressly said she lacked enough direct insight into either home to recommend which parent should be primary. Do not overstate her as endorsing Eric's custody position.
- Some of Heidi's comments to Eric were labeled by her as "off the record." Their discoverability/admissibility and any professional-ethics issues should be assessed by counsel before trial use.
- Eric's own emails contain accusatory descriptions of Sana and acknowledge that Heidi's characterization of the session as last-minute was not technically accurate. Preserve this because it may be used to challenge context or credibility.
- Sana's stated reason for changing counselors was concern over Eric's influence and Heidi's availability; that competing explanation must be presented alongside Heidi's interpretation.

This is one of the strongest external-evidence clusters located so far. Its strongest use is not "Heidi thought Eric should have custody"—she explicitly refused to say that. Its strongest use is that a neutral treating counselor contemporaneously documented Rayan feeling caught in parental conflict, wanting relationships with both parents, and objecting to negative statements about his father; then the counselor was discontinued shortly after refusing a request to recommend a custody arrangement favoring Sana.
