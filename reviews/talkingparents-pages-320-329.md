# TalkingParents review — pages 320–329

Source: certified TalkingParents record, pp.320–329. Record is grouped by subject, not strict chronology.

## Key findings

### 4/9/2023 — discipline / face-slapping dispute
Sana accuses Eric of teaching Rayan disrespect toward her and undermining discipline. Eric responds with extensive personal/psychological commentary, but also repeatedly narrows the practical issue to whether face-slapping is occurring and asks to discuss alternatives. He says: “We agree its happening so why cant we talk about it?” and later apologizes for using the word “abuse” if it was triggering and asks to “come to a solution together.”

Significance: mixed. Useful for showing Eric sought a substantive discipline discussion and alternatives, but the tone is highly inflammatory and includes “Do you love Rayan anymore?”, “litigant,” “come back,” and similar language that is adverse communication evidence.

### 4/3/2023 — missed reading bingo / backpack work
Sana identifies specific school assignments not completed during Eric’s possession, including reading bingo due 4/3. Eric acknowledges remembering the bingo activity and says he will “work harder to check the backpack everyday.”

Significance: clear Eric adverse fact because he effectively concedes the school-work miss. He then dilutes the accountability with hostile commentary about Sana’s emotional issues and parenting.

### 4/9–4/11/2023 — school follow-through
Sana documents that both children completed reading bingo and other work during her possession. Eric later reports Rayan finished the geometry project and attaches it, and says Rayan leveled up in Lexia.

Significance: balanced evidence that both parents were engaged in school tasks; does not support a clean “one parent cares / one does not” narrative.

### 4/9/2023 — clothes / supplies dispute
Sana says the children lack properly fitting clothes/underwear at Eric’s residence and attaches receipts. Eric says Rayan had 11 properly sized pairs of underwear plus more in laundry, offers to send/overstock clothes, but responds with mocking and accusatory language.

Significance: allegation remains disputed; Eric gives a specific factual rebuttal on underwear inventory, but tone is poor. Receipts show Sana did purchase items, not that Eric lacked them.

### 9/18/2022–11/1/2022 — phone / Switch / parental-controls dispute
Sana says Eric bought phone/Switch without co-parenting and withheld parental controls; Eric says he had invited her to management roles and could re-add her. He later accuses her of keeping the devices and characterizes it as theft. Sana later alleges she has seen Eric pushing/shoving children and calls for a custody evaluation.

Significance: longstanding pre-cutoff/post-cutoff technology and control dispute. Serious physical-discipline allegation against Eric is uncorroborated in this block and should be preserved as allegation, not fact.

### 10/2022 — contact restriction / grounding narrative
Eric reports Rayan is effectively grounded from phone/Fortnite and tells Sana he interprets the restriction as alienation. In a particularly risky message, Eric says he tells Rayan that if he is confused about punishment it may be because mom is angry at dad, and contrasts legitimate discipline with Sana’s “fighty words.”

Significance: major adverse alienation-optics item. Even if Eric’s underlying concern about contact restrictions is valid, explaining mom’s discipline to the child as anger toward dad risks undermining her authority and can be used against Eric.

### 10/30/2022 — phone call after Fortnite
Eric gives a concrete example: Sana said five more minutes, Eric ended the Fortnite game, then Rayan remained only on the phone with Eric; Eric says Rayan was then grounded for not getting off “technology.” Sana responds that Eric is micromanaging her possession time.

Significance: useful fact pattern supporting the narrower theory that father-child phone contact may have been treated as part of “technology” restriction. Still only Eric’s account in this thread; call logs / child messages would strengthen it.

### 3/10–3/11/2023 — Facebook
Eric asks why Sana put Rayan on Facebook; Sana says Rayan already had Facebook/Messenger because Eric set it up earlier without her consent.

Significance: mixed technology-joint-decision evidence. No clean winner.

## Best favorable item
Eric’s 4/9/23 message eventually shifts from accusation to a concrete co-parenting ask: he apologizes for using triggering language and asks to discuss discipline, anger, and alternatives to face-slapping together.

## Strongest adverse item
Eric’s 10/25/22 message to Sana says he tells Rayan that when punishment makes no sense it may be because mom is upset with dad, and labels some of her discipline “fighty words.” This creates substantial parental-undermining / alienation optics even if Eric believed he was protecting Rayan emotionally.
