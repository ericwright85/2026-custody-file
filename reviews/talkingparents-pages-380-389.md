# TalkingParents review — pages 380–389

Source: `talking parents record(3).pdf`, pages 380–389.

## High-value findings

### 1) 10/13/2022 — Sana ties continued 50% possession to parenting performance
Sana writes: “If you want to keep your 50% possession time I suggest you be a responsible adult co-parent…” in the context of Aleena’s homework. This is important because it predates later modification efforts and frames possession as conditional on Sana’s assessment of Eric’s parenting.

Source locator: p384; current file-read lines 164–167.

### 2) 10/24/2022 — Rayan had strep after Eric said he was “not sick”
Sana reported runny nose/sore throat after exchange; Eric answered “He’s not sick” and accused her of pretending. Later that day she reported a positive strep test and a 10-day antibiotic course. Eric then acknowledged no symptoms were apparent to him and shifted to a dosing concern.

Significance: concrete adverse fact for Eric. It weakens blanket claims that Sana’s illness reports were fabricated, at least in this instance.

Source locator: pp382–383; lines 97–137.

### 3) 10/12–10/18/2022 — Aleena homework dispute is mixed, not one-sided
Sana says Aleena had not completed homework during Eric’s week. Eric initially says he will look into it, then escalates badly. A few days later he provides a calendar showing work checked off through 10/21 and says additional work was already completed. Sana responds that Aleena had been caught up during her possession and still needs routine.

Significance: do not characterize as clean proof either parent neglected school. It shows active school involvement by both, with high-conflict framing.

Source locator: pp383–387; lines 139–299.

### 4) 10/17/2022 — school absence marked by mistake
Sana asks why Rayan was absent. She later reports the teacher had marked him absent by mistake and corrected it.

Significance: useful example of an accusation/problem resolving objectively and should not be counted as an Eric attendance failure.

Source locator: p388; lines 323–333.

### 5) 10/10/2022 — sick-child communication dispute
Sana says both kids were clearly sick at transfer and that Eric gave no notice. Later she reports Rayan tested positive for strep and Aleena was prescribed antibiotics pending lab verification. Eric’s response to the accusation was mostly tone-focused rather than a factual medical answer.

Significance: another adverse communication/medical optics point for Eric; underlying timing of symptoms remains partly uncertain.

Source locator: p389; lines 360–383.

## Eric adverse communication
This block contains several harsh and diagnostic-style messages: “get help,” threats about losing possession, allegations of abuse/narcissism, and repeated psychologizing. These are significant litigation optics and should be preserved rather than minimized.

Most important example: after Sana says “If you want to keep your 50% possession time…,” Eric replies that she is the one at risk of losing possession due to alleged abuse, then expands into extensive accusations about her mental state and parenting.

## Theme links
- Material-change continuity / modification agenda: Sana explicitly conditions keeping 50% possession in October 2022.
- School/education: mixed; both parents engaged, with a real dispute over routine and completion.
- Medical communication: two adverse examples for Eric where illness concerns were initially discounted.
- Conflict-generation thesis: this block is too mixed to support a clean one-sided thesis; Eric’s own tone materially contributes to conflict.
