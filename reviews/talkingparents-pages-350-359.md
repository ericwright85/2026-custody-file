A long December 2022 dispute centers on whether standard week-on/week-off possession resumes immediately after the Christmas/New Year holiday period. Eric repeatedly states that when holiday possession ends, standard possession resumes, which would give him the children through Jan. 8. Caution: Eric's tone throughout the dispute is frequently insulting and unnecessary (e.g. reading-comprehension insults, "dumbass mistake," claims Sana is acting like an idiot), which is a real adverse communication fact even where his substantive interpretation appears supported.

### School-project / funeral absence is mixed and potentially adverse
Sana asks what progress Rayan made on a Texas Missions project. Eric initially focuses on Sana's phrasing and says the project is supposed to be done at school. Sana later says Rayan arrived with no progress and had also missed two school days for a family funeral trip to Kentucky. Eric explains the extraordinary family circumstance — recovery and burial of Great Uncle Bobby after 72 years missing from the Korean War — and criticizes Sana's framing. Sana then says Eric should have asked for a swap instead of having the children miss school and says Rayan ultimately completed the project and earned a good grade.

This is not a clean positive for either parent. Preserve as mixed: legitimate extraordinary family reason, but missed school and no project progress can still be argued as an educational-management adverse fact.
