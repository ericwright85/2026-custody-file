## Key messages

### Conditional love / negative-child framing
In one screenshot Rayan writes from Aleena's phone: `Hi this is ray mom just said that when I’m being bad she doesn’t love me just wanted to let you know love you`.

Significance:
- Direct child report of conditional-love language tied to being "bad."
- Closely parallels the older journal evidence in which Rayan described being called a "bad seed" and feeling unloved.
- Stronger as longitudinal pattern evidence than as proof of the exact adult wording absent independent corroboration.

### Punitive isolation / communications restriction
In another screenshot Rayan says his mother had been "grumpy" since the prior night after a discipline issue, removed Wi-Fi access, imposed time limits, uninstalled FaceTime/camera, and that he could no longer call from Alexa. He says he could not receive Eric's reply.

Significance:
- Shows Rayan experiencing discipline as broad communication/device restriction.
- Some restrictions may be ordinary parenting consequences; the potentially important issue is whether restrictions also cut off reasonable contact with the other parent or are paired with fear/rejection language.
- This screenshot alone does not establish improper motive.

### Fear / rejection language
In a later iMessage screenshot, Rayan reports that his mother took his other phone, went through it, was "horrible," and after he yelled back told him, `get out I don’t want to talk to you`. He then says she does not know he has the second phone, that she would be angry if she knew, and `I’m lowkey scared`; when Eric asks what he is scared of, he answers `Mom` and then describes the home in extremely negative terms.

Significance:
- Direct contemporaneous child communication describing fear of his mother and a rejection-style interaction.
- This does not independently prove why the interaction occurred or whether Rayan's description is complete, but it is highly relevant to the claimed home-emotional-environment hypothesis.
- Eric's visible response begins with sympathy and a wish for "peace and love" rather than prompting allegations.

### Physical-discipline allegation
In another screenshot Rayan writes from Aleena's phone that he is grounded because his mother `slapped me like a LOT like 15 times in that moment`, while acknowledging he spilled some hot tea on her. He says she took his phone and tells Eric not to reply.

Significance:
- Direct child report of repeated slapping during a discipline incident.
- Rayan includes a fact unfavorable to himself (spilling hot tea), which may matter when assessing spontaneity/completeness, but this is still a child report, not independent proof of the number or nature of contacts.
- The evidence should be compared to any admissions/denials in TalkingParents, medical records, photos, witness accounts, or later counselor reports.

## Longitudinal pattern emerging

Taken together with the young-Rayan journal pages and Heidi Zimmerman evidence, these screenshots support a recurring child-experience pattern rather than a single isolated complaint:

1. negative identity labeling / "bad" framing;
2. perceived withdrawal of love or rejection when in trouble;
3. fear of anger/punishment;
4. communications restriction or secrecy around contacting Eric;
5. child reporting physical discipline;
6. child describing distress while still seeking connection with both parents.

This pattern can be relevant to Rayan's later school behavior as a **competing causation hypothesis**. It does not by itself establish that Sana's parenting caused the discipline problems.

## Evidentiary limits

- Most screenshots do not display a full calendar date; file-modified dates are not substitutes.
- These are direct statements by Rayan to Eric, but still require admissibility analysis and, where possible, corroboration.
- Context immediately before each message is incomplete.
- Normal parental discipline (screen limits, device restrictions, grounding) should not be conflated with emotional maltreatment without the surrounding context.
- Exact wording/number of alleged slaps should be treated as Rayan's report unless corroborated.
