## Emerging theme additions
1. **Custody leverage from ordinary parenting disputes** — threats or references to losing/keeping 50% arise around homework, technology, daycare, etc.
2. **Objective-record correction** — teacher/medical/school records sometimes materially change the characterization made in TalkingParents.
3. **Eric repeatedly invites mother/family inclusion** — lunches, dinners, birthdays, one-on-one time, co-parenting counseling.
4. **Bad-fact discipline** — keep incidents where Eric’s response/tone or judgment may help Sana; do not build an advocacy-only index.
5. **Parent-child communication access** — recurring disputes about calls/devices/contact may be testable with objective logs.
6. **“My time” language** — possession is sometimes framed as a parent’s exclusive entitlement even when shared attendance/contact would not alter the schedule.
