# TalkingParents review — pp. 920–929

## High-value findings

### 1/29–2/5/2020 sleep-schedule dispute
Eric repeatedly asks “What changed?” and states that the schedule had been working for months. He offers to absorb disruption himself rather than change the children’s routines and later proposes a concrete 6am/8pm consistency plan. This is useful pre-cutoff support for the themes that the existing schedule was functioning and that Eric preferred continuity over parent-driven restructuring.

Sana gives a concrete rationale: her commute/work schedule and the children’s difficulty waking early after Eric’s possession. By 2/5 the parties converge on trying a 6am wake / 8pm bedtime weekday routine. This is a good example of eventual functional co-parenting after conflict.

### 1/17/2020 scout garden cleanup
Eric asks whether Sana plans to attend and, if work prevents it, asks whether he may take Rayan. This supports involvement and an effort to preserve the child’s participation rather than treat possession time as a barrier.

### 11/24/2019 helmet dispute
Sana says Eric needs to use a helmet; Eric says he had already instructed Rayan to put it on before the photo. The thread does not independently resolve whose version is correct. Treat as disputed safety allegation, not proof of unsafe parenting.
