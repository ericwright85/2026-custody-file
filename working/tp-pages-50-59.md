# TalkingParents sequential review — pp. 50–59

Source: `talking parents record(3).pdf`

## pp. 50–52 — Father-daughter dance: Sana affirmatively facilitates Eric/Aleena time
- Sana sends the father-daughter dance information, tells Eric he “need[s] to take Aleena,” offers pickup from her home during her possession, suggests dinner/flowers, and later gives a 4pm pickup time.
- Eric accepts, takes Aleena, and returns her after the dance.
- Theme: **important balanced evidence / Sana supports father-daughter relationship**.
- Caution: Sana also asks when the time will be given back, showing the recurring parent-time accounting pattern even around a child-centered event.

## p. 52 — university tours: ordinary cooperative planning
- Sana proposes university tours for both children and explicitly invites Eric to be involved; Eric says he is interested.
- Theme: **positive co-parenting evidence / shared educational involvement**.

## pp. 52–53 — wellness appointment scheduling
- Sana schedules both children for Bluefish wellness visits and tells Eric he can adjust the appointment because it falls on his time, asking only to be informed.
- Eric checks the custody calendar and asks whether she instead wants it moved to his following week.
- Theme: **ordinary medical coordination; useful balance against claims of categorical inability to communicate**.

## pp. 53–54 — two-hour schedule trade successfully negotiated
- Sana asks for the children two hours early on 12/7 and offers Eric two compensating hours on another day.
- After some back-and-forth they reach a clear agreement: Eric keeps them until 8:15pm on 11/21; Sana gets them at 4pm on 12/7.
- Theme: **schedule can work through concrete reciprocal trades**.
- This is useful counterevidence to any claim that the possession arrangement itself is inherently unworkable.

## pp. 54–55 — time with children repeatedly accounted for as repayable units
- Sana asks Eric to watch Aleena for five hours while she has one-on-one time with Rayan, then says she wants the five hours “back” specifically with Aleena.
- Eric says the children have told both parents they like flexibility to move between homes and says he supports that.
- Sana rejects treating prior flexibility as credit and threatens to cancel the arrangement unless she receives replacement time.
- Theme: **parent-time accounting vs child flexibility**.

## pp. 55–56 — Rayan orthodontic care: documented involvement, with one attendance issue
- Eric documented the 2024 dental cleaning, sealant, orthodontic consult, care plan and cost estimate; the dentist reportedly said waiting to begin braces would not change treatment length, and Rayan preferred to wait.
- By September 2025 Eric had begun orthodontic treatment, communicated the expander protocol and follow-up timing, and offered to reschedule around Sana’s week.
- Sana later asks why Rayan missed an entire school day for a single orthodontic appointment; the thread does not contain Eric’s explanation.
- Eric later says they are behind on expander turns and cancels a follow-up rather than risk a timing conflict, suggesting Sana schedule the following week or he can schedule later.
- Theme: **mixed medical/attendance fact: substantial orthodontic involvement, but missed school day and treatment-delay details require context**.

## pp. 56–57 — STEM event: allegation followed by compliance

- Theme: **same-facts/different-framing; categorical “lazy/uninvolved” accusation followed by objective completion of requested parenting task**.

## pp. 57–58 — ordinary fall 2025 week-swap negotiation
