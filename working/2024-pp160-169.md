- 1/27/24 Sana asks where Rayan sleeps and whether he has his own room.
- Eric initially refuses to answer directly and frames the question as litigation/pretense. This is a communication bad fact because the underlying question is facially concrete and answerable.

### 4) KRK / school transportation dispute

### 6) Cafeteria balance

## Overall
This block is more mixed than affirmative. Best value is: (a) another explicit 2024 statement that Eric "doesn't deserve" the existing schedule; (b) practical evidence that Eric repeatedly asked for Sana's preferred transportation option and offered accommodation; and (c) the room/sleeping allegation should remain a dedicated adverse/verification item rather than be argued away.
