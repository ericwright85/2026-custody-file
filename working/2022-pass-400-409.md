## High-value favorable / continuity points

### 4/10/2022 — illness communication existed
Eric contemporaneously told Sana Aleena threw up that morning, with no fever or other symptoms. When Sana later claimed he failed to communicate illnesses, the thread itself contains the earlier notice. Source p400.

### 7/29/2022–8/9/2022 — explicit anti-triangulation / child-emotional-burden framing
Eric repeatedly says adult conflict should be discussed with adults only, not placed on Rayan; children of divorce may internalize parental conflict; he wants a solution so the children do not carry the burden of parents not getting along. Strong thematic support for child-centered intent, but mixed with accusatory assumptions about Sana. Source pp401–402.

### 6/8/2022 — Rayan directly asks to return to therapist Heidi
Eric records Rayan saying: “I wanna go to Heidi because I want to talk to her and I haven't seen her in a long time and I want to see her again.” This is direct child-voice evidence supporting Eric’s position that the child wanted continuity with that therapist. Sana instead proposed mutually selecting another therapist from Dr. Meadows’s referral list. Underlying reasons for therapist change are not established in this block. Source pp402–404.

### 6/21/2022 — Eric offers co-parent counseling and face-to-face meetings
Eric says he is willing to pay for co-parent counseling, attend jointly or separately, and have regular face-to-face meetings to assess whether they can rebuild a cooperative relationship. Useful evidence that he proposed non-litigation process options, though surrounded by substantial hostile/psychological language. Source pp403–404.

### 5/3/2022 and follow-up — wellness care and hearing recheck
Eric reports taking both children to wellness visits, getting Aleena missed vaccines, noticing a hearing-screen issue, then later reports a recheck that she passed. Clean medical follow-through. Source p404.

### 4/22–4/24/2022 — detailed illness updates and medication follow-through
Eric provides a multi-day chronology of Rayan’s fever/vomiting, pediatric visit, testing, school decision, and pharmacy logistics, including finding an open pharmacy and bringing medication that evening. Strong medical-communication evidence. Source pp405–406.

### 4/3–4/5/2022 — repeated effort to create joint family birthday events
Eric asks to have combined family dinners because Rayan reportedly wanted both sides of the family together, offers to pay, and invites Sana and extended family. This echoes earlier “support mom/family relationship” theme. Source pp407 and earlier pp398–399.

### Summer 2021/2022 — flexibility and direct agreement behavior
On 5/9/2021 Eric proposes specific August-week alternatives and suspending Wednesday swaps for summer; in March 2022 the parties exchange written July/August week selections and Sana confirms Eric’s August week. Useful for flexibility / schedule-administration theme. Source pp407–409.

## Adverse / mixed points

### 7/29/2022 and 8/8/2022 — discipline dispute contains assumptions stated as fact
Eric frames Sana’s grounding of Rayan as punishment for her frustration with Eric and as “parental isolation,” based partly on Rayan’s report and Eric’s interpretation. Some underlying facts may be real, but the causal/psychological conclusions are not independently established here. Source pp401–402.

### 6/21/2022 — therapist dispute escalates into unverified motive attribution
Eric says Sana selected/removes therapists for future legal action and “war games,” and says trauma clouds her judgment. Even while advocating continuity for Rayan, this is significant hostile/diagnostic language and motive attribution unsupported by independent evidence in this block. Source pp403–404.

### 4/3/2022 summer-camp discussion — unnecessary hostility
Eric answers a practical summer-camp question with “preoccupied with another one of your games.” Bad tone despite an otherwise ordinary scheduling/childcare discussion. Source p406.

### 4/3/2022 kindergarten registration — dismissive tone
Eric responds that he is aware of Aleena’s age and registration procedures and asks Sana to respond to “more relevant conversations.” This can be used as evidence of poor communication style even though he pivots to the birthday-family proposal. Source p407.

### 4/20–5/12/2021 — post-cutoff July-week deadline dispute
Eric says because Sana did not respond before the July deadline, neither would take summer July weeks and they would use standard schedule; Sana says they had proposed compromises through counsel. This is immediately post-cutoff and useful mainly as continuity of schedule/decree interpretation conflict, not as a clean favorable item. Source pp407–408.

### p409 — severe allegations and credential/account details

The source also displays email account identifiers; do not reproduce or amplify sensitive credentials/details beyond what is necessary for evidence indexing. Source p409.
