## Emerging high-value point
By July/August 2023, Sana was already explicitly seeking standard possession, saying Eric never deserved 50%, invoking emotional abuse, child-custody evaluation, safety concerns, technology issues, school/event involvement, and alienation claims. This is potentially powerful **pre-2026 continuity evidence** against portraying those categories as newly arisen material changes.

A second important counterpoint is now equally clear: Eric’s historic messages contain substantial hostile psychological commentary about Sana, including some direct discussion of those ideas with Rayan. We should build the case around **observable conduct and objective evidence**, not defend every old message. The strongest answer is likely Eric’s repeated concrete conduct supporting contact, inclusion, respect for mom, flexibility, and his explicit reluctance to reduce her custody.
