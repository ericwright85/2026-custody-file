## Late-2023 synthesis
The record increasingly supports two simultaneous truths: (1) Sana’s modification/safety/alienation/education/fitness theory was already explicit by 2023, and (2) Eric had several real vulnerabilities—hostile messaging, refusal to answer some legitimate factual questions, and potentially serious supervision/safety allegations.
