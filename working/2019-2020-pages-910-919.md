She also raises concerns about Eric’s nightlife, gambling/poker, marijuana, and being out late; Eric disputes the characterization but does not squarely deny marijuana in this exchange. Keep as adverse allegation, not established fact.

### 1/29/2020 child preference / transportation
Eric proposes letting Rayan choose between the bus and before-school Club Rewind. This is an early example of Eric emphasizing child preference. However, giving a young child the deciding role in a parent logistics dispute can also be criticized as over-delegating adult decisions.
