## Current provisional classification

### Strongest continuity categories
- opposition to 50/50 / desire for primary or standard possession
- poor co-parenting / communication
- possession and exchange conflict
- safety criticism as a category
- substance-use / gambling allegations as categories
- school / medical / decision-making disputes as categories
- mutual allegations of disparagement and involving children in adult conflict

### Highest-risk potentially new categories
- **confirmed:** objective post-cutoff school discipline / behavior deterioration by 2025–26; causation and remedy remain unresolved
- adolescent behavioral / mental-health developments
- a serious corroborated new safety event
- a sustained new pattern of violating joint rights or orders
- new evaluator/therapist recommendations based on changed circumstances
- a mature child's legally relevant preference, depending on context and governing law

## Working rule
Do not answer a broad allegation with a broad denial. Break it into dated factual propositions, classify each as **pre-existing / new / disputed / corroborated / disproved / unresolved**, then connect it to actual child impact and the requested remedy.
