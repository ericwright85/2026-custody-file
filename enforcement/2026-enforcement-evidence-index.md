## Rayan orthodontic chart — missed appointments during both 2025 and 2026 withholding weeks

**Source:** [`2026 - rayan ortho - showing sana missed during violation week.pdf`](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1X-57MBrlZV8UHyxrvzjP0cgWHev5ZnmJ/view)

This is a provider-generated treatment chart from Dr. Daniel Le / Cypress Rosehill orthodontic practice.

### August 4, 2025

The chart records **8/04/2025 — `no show, sent text`**. August 4 falls inside the disputed **8/3–8/10/2025** week that Eric says was his designated week and during which the TalkingParents record places Sana and the children in Colorado.

The chart later contains an **8/25/2025** note referring to the missed appointment / Mom calling about the missed appointment.

**What it proves:** an orthodontic appointment was missed on 8/4/2025.

**What it does not prove by itself:** why it was missed, who knew about it, or who bore legal responsibility for transportation.

### August 6, 2026

The chart records a missed **8/06/2026** appointment and includes office notes substantially stating:

- `pt missed appt`
- `per dad pt is with his mom missed appt`
- `no show or txt ... appt ...` followed by rescheduling with Dad for August 17
- Dad requested a record of the missed 8/6 appointment.

The chart also contains a **5/27/2026** note that the office left Mom a voicemail about an appointment / rescheduling. Before using that note to prove notice of the August 6 appointment, counsel should confirm that the note actually refers to the same appointment.

**What it proves:** the orthodontic provider's own chart documents an 8/6/26 missed appointment during the disputed August 2–9 period and records that Dad told the office Rayan was with Mom.

**What it does not independently prove:** that Sana knowingly caused the missed appointment or had actual notice of the specific 8/6 appointment. The `with his mom` attribution is recorded by the provider but sourced to Eric.

### Pattern value

The coincidence is potentially useful and should be presented carefully: **both disputed/withheld August weeks, one year apart, contain provider-documented orthodontic no-shows for Rayan.** This is stronger as concrete child-impact/context evidence than a generalized assertion that schedule violations are harmful, but responsibility for each missed appointment still requires proof.

## Bottom-line factual model

The strongest current enforcement presentation is **not** `Sana gets every calendar wrong.` The record contains genuine ambiguities and even examples where Eric revised his own interpretation after counsel review.

The narrower, better-supported model is:

> Sana repeatedly asserted August 2–9 despite earlier written designations identifying different 2026 periods; Eric proposed a swap but later expressly stated no agreement had been reached; the August 2 exchange did not occur; Sana continued to act as though August 2–9 belonged to her and told Eric he did not deserve the time and could file; the children remained with her during the disputed week; and Rayan's provider chart documents a missed orthodontic appointment during that period.
